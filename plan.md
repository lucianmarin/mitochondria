# Book Plan: Mitochondria — The Architects of Life, Mind, and Longevity

## Chapter 1: Beyond the Powerhouse: The Mitochondrial Paradigm Shift
- From static ATP factories to the **Mitochondrial Information Processing System (MIPS)** (Martin Picard & Orian Shirihai)
- The dual-genome organism and **MitoCarta 3.0** (Vamsi Mootha's 1,136-protein human mitochondrial proteome)
- Mitochondrial diversity across eukaryotes: hydrogenosomes, mitosomes, and *Monocercomonoides*
- The bioenergetic paradigm of human health, chronic disease, and aging (Douglas C. Wallace)

## Chapter 2: Unveiling the Inner Universe: A History of Mitochondrial Science
- Early microscopy: Kölliker's sarcosomes (1857), Altmann's bioblasts (1890), Benda's "mitochondria" (1898), and Janus Green B (1900)
- Biochemical breakthroughs: Warburg's respiratory enzyme, Krebs's citric acid cycle (1937), Claude's cell fractionation, Palade & Sjöstrand's electron microscopy (1952), and Lehninger & Kennedy's localization of OXPHOS (1948–1949)
- The OxPhos Wars and Peter Mitchell's chemiosmotic revolution (1961), followed by Boyer and Walker's rotary ATP synthase mechanism
- The genomic and modern eras: Nass & Nass (1963), Sanger's human mtDNA sequence (1981), Wallace's pathogenic mtDNA mutations (1988), Mootha's MCU discovery (2011), Youle's PINK1/Parkin mitophagy, and Liu's mtDNA base editors (2020–2024)

## Chapter 3: The Ancient Pact: Endosymbiosis and Eukaryotic Evolution
- The Great Oxidation Event and the origin of eukaryotes (~1.8–2.0 billion years ago)
- **Asgard Archaea** (Lokiarchaeota, Hodarchaeales) and Eukaryotic Signature Proteins (ESPs)
- Symbiogenesis models: The **Hydrogen Hypothesis** (William Martin & Miklós Müller) vs. aerobic/syntrophic models
- Endosymbiotic Gene Transfer (EGT), somatic **NUMTs**, and the **Co-location for Redox Regulation (CoRR) Hypothesis** (John F. Allen & Nick Lane)
- The bioenergetic dividend (Nick Lane) and **mitonuclear co-evolution** in speciation

## Chapter 4: Architecture at the Nanoscale: Membranes, Cristae, and Supercomplexes
- The four mitochondrial compartments (OMM, IMS, IMM, Matrix) and their physicochemical properties
- **Cardiolipin** biosynthesis, remodeling via **Tafazzin (TAZ)**, and proton-trap dynamics
- **MICOS** (MIC60, MIC10), **OPA1**, and **ATP synthase dimer rows** shaping cristae curvature
- Cristae as electrically independent bioenergetic units (Orian Shirihai)
- The **Plasticity Model of Respirasomes** (Complexes I, III₂, IV₁₋₂, SCAF1) and the 55S mammalian **mitoribosome**
- Inter-organelle contact sites: MAMs (ER-mitochondria), mitochondria-lysosome, and peridroplet mitochondria

## Chapter 5: The Currency of Life: Bioenergetics, Chemiosmosis, and Metabolic Flux
- Fuel entry: Glycolysis, **Mitochondrial Pyruvate Carrier (MPC1/MPC2)**, PDC, and **CPT1/CPT2** fatty acid $\beta$-oxidation
- The **TCA Cycle** as an amphibolic hub (cataplerosis and anaplerosis) and NADH shuttles
- Electron Transport Chain mechanics (Complexes I–IV), the Q-cycle, and **Reverse Electron Transport (RET)** (Michael Murphy & Edward Chouchani)
- Proton-motive force ($\Delta p$), rotary catalysis of **Complex V (ATP Synthase)**, ANT, and the **Creatine Kinase Shuttle**
- Thermogenesis: **UCP1** in brown fat and UCP1-independent **futile creatine and calcium cycling** (Bruce Spiegelman & Lawrence Kazak)

## Chapter 6: The Signaling Hub: Calcium, Metabolites, Stress Responses, and Cell Fate
- **Calcium Homeostasis:** The **MCU holocomplex** (MCU, EMRE, MICU1/2/3, MCUb — Vamsi Mootha & Rosario Rizzuto)
- **Redox Signaling and Mitohormesis:** Superoxide/$\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ microdomains and Michael Ristow's human mitohormesis studies
- **TCA Metabolites as Epigenetic Regulators** (Navdeep Chandel): Acetyl-CoA, $\alpha$-ketoglutarate, succinate, fumarate, and 2-HG
- **The OMA1–DELE1–HRI Pathway and $\text{ISR}^{\text{mt}}$ / $\text{UPR}^{\text{mt}}$:** ATF4/ATF5 activation and systemic mitokines (**FGF21** and **GDF15**, Anu Suomalainen-Wartiovaara)
- **The Cell Danger Response (CDR):** Robert Naviaux's framework of purinergic signaling (eATP) and metabolic defense
- **Cell Death Modalities:** Intrinsic **Apoptosis** (BAX/BAK MOMP), the **mPTP**, and **Ferroptosis** (GPX4 and DHODH)

## Chapter 7: The Second Genome: Mitochondrial DNA, Peptides, and Epigenetics
- Human mtDNA structure (16,569 bp), **TFAM** nucleoid packaging, and the replisome (POLG, Twinkle, mtSSB)
- **Mitochondrial-Derived Peptides (MDPs)** (Pinchas Cohen & Changhan David Lee): **Humanin**, **MOTS-c**, and **SHLP1–6**
- Maternal inheritance, paternal mtDNA elimination, **heteroplasmy**, the **genetic bottleneck**, and germline purifying selection
- **mtDNA Haplogroups** (Douglas Wallace) in human migration, climate adaptation, and disease susceptibility
- Ongoing somatic **NUMTogenesis** in the human brain and cancer (Martin Picard & Patrick Chinnery)

## Chapter 8: The Dynamic Network: Biogenesis, Fission, Fusion, Mitophagy, and Intercellular Transfer
- **Mitochondrial Biogenesis:** The **PGC-1$\alpha$ / AMPK / SIRT1 / NRF1-2** network and multi-pathway protein import (TOM, TIM23, TIM22, MIA40)
- **Fusion and Dual-Mode Fission:** MFN1/2, OPA1, and the distinction between **midzone fission** (DRP1-MFF, ER/actin-mediated for biogenesis) and **peripheral fission** (DRP1-FIS1, lysosome-mediated for mitophagy)
- **Quality Control Spectrum:** Proteases, **Mitochondria-Derived Vesicles (MDVs)** (Heidi McBride), **PINK1/Parkin mitophagy** (Richard Youle), receptor-mediated mitophagy (BNIP3, NIX, FUNDC1), USP30 inhibitors, and **mitocytosis**
- **Axonal Transport and Intercellular Transfer:** Miro1/TRAK transport and organelle sharing via **Tunneling Nanotubes (TNTs)**

## Chapter 9: Exercise, Metabolic Flexibility, and Specialized Tissues
- **Skeletal Muscle Bioenergetics and the Lactate Shuttle** (George Brooks & Iñigo San Millán): Mitochondrial Lactate Oxidation Complex (mLOC)
- **Zone 2 Training vs. HIIT:** Slow-twitch mitochondrial biogenesis, **FATmax**, MCT1 lactate clearance, and **peridroplet vs. cytoplasmic mitochondria**
- **Insulin Resistance, Metabolic Gridlock, and MASLD** (Gerald Shulman & Deborah Muoio): Ectopic DAGs/ceramides and incomplete fatty acid oxidation
- **Tissue-Specific "Mitotypes"** (Martin Picard): Specialized mitochondrial architectures in heart, liver, kidney, retina, adrenal cortex, and oocytes

## Chapter 10: Brain Energy, Neurodegeneration, and Metabolic Psychiatry
- **The Brain's Bioenergetic Tightrope:** Synaptic ATP demand and the Astrocyte-Neuron Lactate Shuttle (ANLS)
- **Neurodegenerative Diseases:** Parkinson's disease (Complex I, PINK1/Parkin, $\alpha$-synuclein), Alzheimer's disease (Swerdlow's Bioenergetic Cascade, MAM dysfunction, mitophagy deficit), ALS, and Huntington's disease
- **Mitochondrial Psychobiology** (Martin Picard): Psychological stress, allostatic load, and cell-free mtDNA (cf-mtDNA)
- **Metabolic Psychiatry and the Brain Energy Theory** (Chris Palmer & Shebani Sethi): Mitochondrial dysfunction in schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and depression, and **ketogenic metabolic therapy**

## Chapter 11: Immunity, Inflammation, Infection, and Cancer
- **Innate Immune Signaling:** Antiviral **MAVS** filaments and sterile inflammation via cytosolic mtDNA (**cGAS-STING**, **NLRP3**, **TLR9**)
- **Immunometabolism** (Luke O'Neill & Erika Pearce): TCA remodeling in macrophages (**succinate** vs. **itaconate** via ACOD1/IRG1) and mitochondrial exhaustion in tumor-infiltrating T-cells
- **Post-Viral Syndromes (Long COVID and ME/CFS):** ER-stress-induced **WASF3** disrupting Complex IV (NIH), post-exertional muscle OXPHOS impairment, and persistent Cell Danger Response
- **Cancer Bioenergetics Beyond Warburg:** Essentiality of TCA/ETC for aspartate and pyrimidine synthesis, **oncometabolites** (D-2-HG in *IDH1/2* gliomas treated with **Vorasidenib**, succinate, fumarate), and nanotube-mediated mitochondrial hijacking

## Chapter 12: When the Powerhouse Fails: Primary Mitochondrial Diseases and Diagnostics
- Epidemiology and dual-genome genetics (~1 in 4,300 adults, Patrick Chinnery & Doug Turnbull)
- Classic mtDNA and nuclear syndromes: **MELAS**, **MERRF**, **LHON**, **Kearns-Sayre**, **Pearson**, **Leigh Syndrome**, **POLG** disorders, **TK2d**, **Barth Syndrome**, and **Friedreich's Ataxia**
- Modern diagnostics: Whole-genome sequencing, heteroplasmy profiling, and serum **FGF21** and **GDF15** biomarkers
- Approved and breakthrough therapies: **Omaveloxolone (Skyclarys)**, **Elamipretide (Forzinity)**, **Idebenone (Raxone)**, **Lenadogene nolparvovec (Lumevoq)**, **deoxynucleoside bypass therapy** (Michio Hirano), and **therapeutic hypoxia** (Vamsi Mootha)

## Chapter 13: Rejuvenating the Inner Universe: Aging, Longevity, and Frontier Therapeutics
- **Reframing the Mitochondrial Theory of Aging:** Replication error clonal expansion vs. ROS, **minority MOMP**, and cGAS-STING-driven **inflammaging**
- **Geroprotective Interventions:**
  - **NAD+ Precursors:** Niacin, NR, NMN, and CD38 inhibition
  - **Mitophagy Inducers:** **Urolithin A** (Johan Auwerx) and **Spermidine**
  - **Redox & Glutathione Restoration:** **GlyNAC** (Rajagopal Sekhar) and **SS-31**
  - **Targeted Uncouplers:** **BAM15** and **HU6**
- **Engineering the Future:**
  - **mtDNA Base Editing:** **DdCBEs** and **TALEDs** (David R. Liu) and heteroplasmy-shifting nucleases (**mtZFNs**, **mitoARCUS**)
  - **Mitochondrial Replacement Therapy (MRT):** Maternal Spindle Transfer and Pronuclear Transfer
  - **Mitochondrial Transplantation:** Autologous and allogeneic organelle transfer (James McCully, Minovia)
- **Conclusion: The Symbiotic Imperative**
