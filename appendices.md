## Appendix A: Comprehensive Glossary of Mitochondrial Biology and Medicine

- **Allotopic Expression**: A gene-therapy strategy (used in *Lumevoq* for LHON) in which a mitochondrial DNA gene is recoded into the universal nuclear genetic code, equipped with a mitochondrial targeting sequence, and expressed in the cell nucleus so its protein product is imported into mitochondria.
- **Amphibolic Pathway**: A metabolic pathway (such as the TCA cycle) that functions both catabolically (oxidizing fuel for energy) and anabolically (providing carbon skeletons for biosynthesis via cataplerosis).
- **Asgard Archaea (Asgardarchaeota)**: The superphylum of archaea (including *Lokiarchaeota* and *Hodarchaeales*) that represents the closest prokaryotic relatives of the eukaryotic host cell and already encodes numerous Eukaryotic Signature Proteins (ESPs).
- **ATP Synthase (Complex V / $F_1F_o$-ATPase)**: The rotary molecular motor embedded along cristae ridges that harnesses the proton-motive force ($\Delta p$) across an 8-subunit $c$-ring to phosphorylate ADP into ATP.
- **Base Editors (DdCBE and TALED)**: CRISPR-free, all-protein programmable gene-editing enzymes constructed from TALE DNA-binding arrays and split double-stranded DNA deaminases (DddA or TadA8e) that convert $\text{C}\cdot\text{G} \to \text{T}\cdot\text{A}$ (DdCBE) or $\text{A}\cdot\text{T} \to \text{G}\cdot\text{C}$ (TALED) directly in mtDNA without double-strand breaks.
- **Cardiolipin ($\text{L}_4\text{CL}$)**: A signature dimeric phospholipid with four fatty acyl chains (remodeled by **tafazzin**) located in the inner mitochondrial membrane; essential for negative cristae curvature, supercomplex stability, and proton trapping.
- **Cell Danger Response (CDR)**: Formulated by Robert Naviaux, the conserved metabolic and purinergic (extracellular ATP) defense program triggered when mitochondria shift from OXPHOS to cellular defense during infection, toxin exposure, or trauma.
- **cGAS–STING Pathway**: An innate immune cytosolic DNA-sensing pathway that detects leaked mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) in the cytoplasm and triggers Type I interferon and sterile inflammation ("inflammaging").
- **Co-location for Redox Regulation (CoRR)**: Proposed by John F. Allen and Nick Lane, the evolutionary principle explaining why mitochondria must retain their own genome (mtDNA) to locally regulate expression of core hydrophobic OXPHOS subunits in immediate response to inner-membrane redox state.
- **Cristae**: Invaginations of the inner mitochondrial membrane sculpted by **MICOS**, **OPA1**, and **ATP synthase dimer rows**; each crista can function as an electrically insulated bioenergetic unit.
- **Elamipretide (Forzinity / SS-31)**: A cardiolipin-binding tetrapeptide that stabilizes inner-membrane curvature and respiratory supercomplexes; granted FDA accelerated approval in September 2025 for Barth syndrome.
- **Heteroplasmy**: The coexistence of two or more genetically distinct mtDNA variants (e.g., mutant and wild-type) within the same cell, tissue, or individual.
- **Itaconate**: An endogenous anti-inflammatory metabolite synthesized from *cis*-aconitate by mitochondrial **ACOD1 (IRG1)** in activated macrophages; inhibits succinate dehydrogenase (SDH) and activates **NRF2**.
- **Lactate Shuttle & mLOC**: Formulated by George Brooks and Iñigo San Millán, the physiological mechanism whereby glycolytic cells export lactate via MCT4 to oxidative cells that import and oxidize it directly inside mitochondria via the **Mitochondrial Lactate Oxidation Complex (mLOC)**.
- **MAMs (Mitochondria-Associated ER Membranes)**: Specialized nanometer-scale tethering zones between the endoplasmic reticulum and outer mitochondrial membrane that mediate $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ transfer and phospholipid biosynthesis.
- **MAVS (Mitochondrial Antiviral-Signaling Protein)**: An outer-membrane prion-like signaling hub activated by cytosolic viral RNA sensors (RIG-I/MDA5) to launch the Type I interferon response.
- **Metabolic Psychiatry (Brain Energy Theory)**: Pioneered by Chris Palmer and Shebani Sethi, the clinical framework recognizing major psychiatric disorders (schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, depression) as neurometabolic disorders driven by circuit-level mitochondrial dysfunction.
- **MICOS (Mitochondrial Contact Site and Cristae Organizing System)**: A multi-subunit protein complex (centered on MIC60 and MIC10) localized at cristae junctions that maintains cristae neck architecture and bridges the inner and outer membranes.
- **Midzone vs. Peripheral Fission**: The two mechanistically distinct modes of DRP1-driven mitochondrial division (Kleele et al., *Nature* 2021): ER/actin-mediated **midzone fission** via **MFF** drives organelle proliferation, whereas lysosome-mediated **peripheral fission** via **FIS1** amputates damaged mitochondrial tips for mitophagy.
- **Minority MOMP**: Partial Mitochondrial Outer Membrane Permeabilization occurring in a small subset of mitochondria without killing the cell, releasing mtDNA to trigger cGAS–STING and cellular senescence.
- **MitoCarta 3.0**: The curated compendium developed by Vamsi Mootha's laboratory cataloging the **1,136 human genes** encoding proteins localized to mitochondria across tissues.
- **Mitochondrial Calcium Uniporter (MCU)**: The inner-membrane $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ channel holocomplex (MCU, EMRE, MICU1/2/3, MCUb) that couples cytosolic calcium spikes to matrix TCA dehydrogenase activation.
- **Mitochondrial-Derived Peptides (MDPs)**: Bioactive microproteins—including **Humanin**, **MOTS-c**, and **SHLP1–6**—encoded by short open reading frames (sORFs) within the 12S and 16S rRNA genes of mtDNA.
- **Mitochondrial Information Processing System (MIPS)**: Proposed by Martin Picard and Orian Shirihai, the systems-biology framework describing how mitochondria sense, integrate, and broadcast metabolic, neuroendocrine, and epigenetic signals.
- **Mitohormesis**: The biological principle whereby mild, transient mitochondrial stress (such as exercise-induced pulses of ROS or $\text{NAD}^+/\text{AMP}$ shifts) activates adaptive cytoprotective pathways (**NRF2**, **PGC-1$\alpha$**, **$\text{ISR}^{\text{mt}}$**) that enhance long-term resilience and longevity.
- **Mitokines (FGF21 and GDF15)**: Systemic endocrine hormones secreted by stressed cells upon activation of the **OMA1–DELE1–HRI–ATF4** mitochondrial integrated stress response ($\text{ISR}^{\text{mt}}$); widely used as clinical serum biomarkers of mitochondrial disease.
- **Mitophagy**: Selective autophagic degradation of damaged or depolarized mitochondria via ubiquitin-dependent (**PINK1–Parkin**) or receptor-mediated (**BNIP3, NIX, FUNDC1**) pathways.
- **NUMTs (Nuclear Mitochondrial DNA Segments)**: Fragments of mtDNA inserted into the nuclear genome over evolutionary time and continuously across the human lifespan (*de novo* somatic NUMTogenesis).
- **Omaveloxolone (Skyclarys)**: An oral **NRF2** activator and the first FDA-approved therapy (2023) for **Friedreich's ataxia**.
- **Oncometabolite**: A TCA-cycle-derived metabolite (**D-2-hydroxyglutarate**, **succinate**, or **fumarate**) that accumulates to millimolar levels due to mutations in *IDH1/2*, *SDH*, or *FH*, competitively inhibiting $\alpha$-ketoglutarate-dependent TET and JmjC epigenetic demethylases.
- **PGC-1$\alpha$ (*PPARGC1A*)**: The master transcriptional coactivator of mitochondrial biogenesis, activated by **AMPK** phosphorylation and **SIRT1** deacetylation.
- **Respirasome**: A dynamic respiratory supercomplex ($\text{I}_1\text{III}_2\text{IV}_{1-2}$) assembling Complexes I, III, and IV in the cristae membrane according to the **Plasticity Model**.
- **Reverse Electron Transport (RET)**: Backward electron flow from a hyper-reduced ubiquinol ($\text{CoQH}_2$) pool through Complex I under high proton-motive force (e.g., driven by succinate oxidation during ischemia-reperfusion), generating localized superoxide bursts.
- **Urolithin A**: A gut-microbiome postbiotic derived from dietary ellagitannins that induces **mitophagy** and improves muscle endurance and T-cell immunity in human clinical trials.
- **Vorasidenib (Voranigo)**: A brain-penetrant dual inhibitor of mutant **IDH1 and IDH2** enzymes; FDA-approved in August 2024 for *IDH*-mutant low-grade gliomas.
- **Zone 2 Training**: Aerobic exercise performed just below the first lactate threshold ($\text{LT}_1$, typically $<2.0\text{ mmol/L}$ blood lactate), maximizing Type I slow-twitch muscle fiber recruitment, mitochondrial biogenesis, $\text{FAT}_{\text{max}}$ fatty acid oxidation, and **MCT1** lactate clearance.

## Appendix C: Leading Researchers, Clinicians, and Landmark Studies

### Pioneers of Mitochondrial Genetics, Proteomics, and Evolution
- **Douglas C. Wallace, PhD (Children's Hospital of Philadelphia):** Maternal inheritance of human mtDNA (1980), first pathogenic mtDNA mutation in LHON (1988), human mtDNA haplogroups, and the bioenergetic paradigm of chronic disease (*Mitochondria*, 2nd Ed.; *JAMA*, *Cell*, *Science*).
- **Vamsi K. Mootha, MD (Harvard Medical School / Broad Institute / HHMI):** Co-creator of **MitoCarta 1.0–3.0** (Rath et al., *Nucleic Acids Research* 2021), discovery of the **Mitochondrial Calcium Uniporter (MCU)** (Baughman et al., *Nature* 2011), aspartate synthesis as the core proliferative function of the ETC (Birsoy et al., *Cell* 2015), and **chronic hypoxia therapy** for Leigh syndrome (Jain et al., *Science* 2016).
- **Nick Lane, PhD (University College London) & William F. Martin, PhD (University of Düsseldorf):** Bioenergetics of eukaryogenesis (*Nature* 2010; *The Vital Question*, 2015; *Transformer*, 2022), the **Hydrogen Hypothesis** (Martin & Müller, *Nature* 1998), and the **CoRR Hypothesis** (with John F. Allen).
- **Patrick F. Chinnery, MD, PhD (University of Cambridge) & Sir Doug M. Turnbull, MD, PhD (Newcastle University):** Epidemiology of mitochondrial disease, the germline genetic bottleneck, ongoing human **NUMTogenesis** (Wei et al., *Nature* 2022), and clinical **Pronuclear Transfer (PNT)** (Hyslop et al., *Nature* 2016).

### Pioneers of Mitochondrial Dynamics, Structure, and Quality Control
- **Richard J. Youle, PhD (National Institutes of Health):** Elucidation of the **PINK1–Parkin** mitophagy pathway and its link to Parkinson's disease and cGAS–STING neuroinflammation (Narendra et al., *JCB* 2008; Sliter et al., *Nature* 2018; 2021 Breakthrough Prize).
- **David C. Chan, MD, PhD (Caltech), Jodi Nunnari, PhD, & Luca Scorrano, MD, PhD (Padua):** Discovery of the fusion/fission machinery (**MFN1/2**, **OPA1**, **DRP1**) and OPA1 control of cristae junctions.
- **Orian S. Shirihai, MD, PhD (UCLA) & Suliana Manley, PhD (EPFL):** Electrical independence of individual cristae (Wolf et al., *EMBO J* 2019) and discovery of **midzone vs. peripheral mitochondrial fission** (Kleele et al., *Nature* 2021).
- **José Antonio Enríquez, PhD (CNIC Madrid), Judy Hirst, PhD (Cambridge), & Leonid Sazanov, PhD (ISTA):** The **Plasticity Model** of respiratory supercomplexes (Acín-Pérez et al., *Molecular Cell* 2008; *Nature* 2016–2024) and atomic cryo-EM mechanisms of Complex I.

### Pioneers of Psychobiology, Metabolic Psychiatry, and Exercise Physiology
- **Martin Picard, PhD (Columbia University):** **Mitochondrial Information Processing System (MIPS)** (Picard & Shirihai, *Cell Metabolism* 2022), **MitoBrainMap** (Mosharov et al., *Nature* 2025), psychological stress-induced cell-free mtDNA, and somatic brain NUMTs.
- **Chris Palmer, MD (Harvard Medical School / McLean Hospital) & Shebani Sethi, MD (Stanford University):** **Brain Energy Theory** of mental illness (*Brain Energy*, 2022) and clinical trials of **ketogenic metabolic therapy** in schizophrenia and bipolar disorder (Sethi et al., *Psychiatry Research* 2024).
- **George A. Brooks, PhD (UC Berkeley) & Iñigo San Millán, PhD (University of Colorado):** The **Lactate Shuttle** and **Mitochondrial Lactate Oxidation Complex (mLOC)** (Brooks, *Cell Metabolism* 2018), **Zone 2 training**, and metabolic flexibility in elite athletes vs. metabolic disease (San Millán & Brooks, *Sports Medicine* 2018).
- **Gerald I. Shulman, MD, PhD (Yale) & Deborah M. Muoio, PhD (Duke):** Ectopic lipid/DAG-induced insulin resistance (*NEJM*, *Physiological Reviews* 2018) and mitochondrial carbon gridlock.

### Pioneers of Signaling, Immunometabolism, Aging, and Therapeutics
- **Anu Suomalainen-Wartiovaara, MD, PhD (University of Helsinki):** **FGF21 and GDF15** as clinical mitokines, the mitochondrial integrated stress response ($\text{ISR}^{\text{mt}}$), and **niacin ($\text{B}_3$)** therapy for adult mitochondrial myopathy (Pirinen et al., *Cell Metabolism* 2020).
- **Navdeep S. Chandel, PhD (Northwestern), Luke A. J. O'Neill, PhD (Trinity College Dublin), & Erika L. Pearce, PhD (Johns Hopkins):** Mitochondria as signaling organelles (*Cell Metabolism*), **itaconate** vs. **succinate** in macrophage immunometabolism (Mills et al., *Nature* 2018), and mitochondrial remodeling in T-cell memory and exhaustion.
- **Pinchas Cohen, MD & Changhan David Lee, PhD (USC):** Discovery of **Mitochondrial-Derived Peptides (MDPs)** including **Humanin**, **MOTS-c**, and **SHLP1–6** (Lee et al., *Cell Metabolism* 2015; Kim et al., *Cell Metabolism* 2018).
- **Robert K. Naviaux, MD, PhD (UC San Diego):** The **Cell Danger Response (CDR)** and purinergic signaling in chronic disease (*Mitochondrion* 2014, 2020).
- **David R. Liu, PhD (Broad Institute / Harvard), Joseph Mougous, PhD, & Jin-Soo Kim, PhD:** Invention of **DdCBE** cytosine base editors (Mok et al., *Nature* 2020) and **TALED** adenine base editors (Cho et al., *Cell* 2022) for mtDNA.
- **Johan Auwerx, MD, PhD (EPFL) & Rajagopal V. Sekhar, MD (Baylor College of Medicine):** Clinical translation of **Urolithin A** mitophagy activation (Ryu et al., *Nature Medicine* 2016; Singh et al., *Cell Reports Medicine* 2022) and **GlyNAC** supplementation in human aging (*Journals of Gerontology* 2023).

## Appendix D: Research Databases, Atlas Tools, and Clinical Resources

- **MitoCarta 3.0 (Broad Institute):** Comprehensive inventory and sub-organellar localization of the 1,136 human and mouse mitochondrial proteins (`broadinstitute.org/mitocarta`).
- **MITOMAP:** Compendium of human mitochondrial DNA polymorphisms, haplogroups, and pathogenic mutations (`mitomap.org`).
- **MitoBrainMap:** High-resolution spatial atlas of mitochondrial density, respiratory complex activity, and mitotypes across the human brain (`mitobrainmap.org`).
- **United Mitochondrial Disease Foundation (UMDF) & The Lily Foundation:** Clinical trial registries, diagnostic guidelines, and patient support networks (`umdf.org`, `thelilyfoundation.org.uk`).
- **Mitochondrial Medicine Society (MMS):** Consensus clinical criteria for the diagnosis, preventive care, and emergency management of primary mitochondrial diseases (`mitomedicine.org`).
